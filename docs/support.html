Common questions

I don't hear beeps when my phone is locked

Make sure the workout was started in the app before locking. Beeps play with the silent switch on and over music. Check the volume slider in Settings → Volume and use Test Sound.

Distance looks wrong

GPS works best outdoors with a clear view of the sky. On a treadmill, choose Settings → Distance → Step counter only. The step counter gets more accurate after a few outdoor GPS runs. If distance isn't measured at all, go to iPhone Settings → Apps → Runwise and turn on Location (While Using the App) and Motion & Fitness.

I didn't get my sign-in code

Check your spam folder and wait a minute before requesting another code. You can also use Sign in with Apple.

How do I change my training plan?

Go to Settings → Edit my answers, then choose Build a new plan.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all of its data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
