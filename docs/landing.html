  1. Answer a few quick questions. About two minutes. You can change your answers any time.
  2. Get your weeks. Sessions only land on your training days and never run longer than you have.
  3. Press Start. Today's run sits at the top of the Plan tab, ready to play in the timer.

Every interval, measured.

See how far and how fast you went in every run and walk, live while you move and as a full breakdown when you finish.

  • Step counter on the treadmill. It takes over wherever GPS is weak, one stretch at a time.
  • Heart rate, if you want it. Pair a Bluetooth chest strap or broadcasting watch, or read it from Apple Health after the run.

AI coach

A coach who reads every run.

After each session, tell your coach how it felt. It looks at your times, paces and heart rate, and suggests changes when your plan needs them.

  • Ask anything. How your training is going, what pace your easy runs should be, or what to do about a week away.
  • You stay in charge. Your coach can suggest changes to your plan. Nothing changes until you accept.
  • Plans that adapt. If runs are harder than they should be, the next ones ease off.

Routes

Three routes. Pick one and go.

Choose a start and a distance. Runwise offers three routes heading different ways, each measured along real roads and paths. Or draw your own, one tap at a time.

  • Parks and trails first. Routes aim for green space near your start, and say what they pass through.
  • Matched to your session. Each plan session suggests saved routes that fit how far it will take you at your usual pace.
  • A live map while you run. Your route is dotted, the ground you've covered is solid.
