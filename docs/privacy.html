Runwise Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026. Updated October 26, 2026 to cover saved routes.

Runwise is a running coach and training-plan app for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and an account ID. If you use Sign in with Apple, we get the email Apple provides, which may be a private relay address.
  • Your onboarding answers: your running goal, current fitness and running, training days, session length, running experience, optional age range, where you run, and any optional notes, including injuries or health conditions you choose to tell us about.
  • Workouts and run history: the workouts you create, and for each run the date, duration, and the distance and pace of each interval.
  • Training plans built for you by the AI coach.
  • Routes you plan: if you draw a route on the map, the points you tap and the path between them are saved to your account, along with the route's name, its length and which plan sessions you've picked it for. This is precise location information, so only save routes you're comfortable storing, for example ones that don't start at your front door.

What stays on your phone

The app uses GPS and the motion step counter during a workout to measure distance. The location and step data recorded during your runs never leave your phone. Only the resulting distances and paces are saved to your run history. The app doesn't track your location when no workout is running. The only location information that leaves your phone is the routes you choose to plan and save, described above.

How we use it

We use your information only to run the app: to sign you in, back up and sync your workouts, history and plans across devices, and build your training plan. We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run the app. Each one receives only what it needs:

  • Supabase hosts the app's database and sign-in. Everything listed under "What we collect" is stored there.
  • Anthropic (Claude) generates your training plan. When you build a plan, and only after you agree in the app, your onboarding answers and a summary of your last 60 days of runs are sent to Anthropic. Your email and name are not sent. Anthropic processes this to produce your plan under its commercial terms, which by default don't allow it to train its models on this data.
  • Resend delivers the sign-in code emails, so it receives your email address.
  • Apple, if you use Sign in with Apple. When you plan a route, its points are also sent to Apple Maps: to find walking paths between them, and, when the app makes a route for you, to find parks and trails near your start. This is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, onboarding answers, workouts, run history, plans and routes from our servers and from your phone. You can also delete a single route any time by swiping it away in the Routes list. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also revoke the app's access to your Apple ID.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules mean each account can only read and change its own data.

Children

Runwise is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Health information

Training plans are generated by AI and are not medical advice. Anything you tell us about injuries or health conditions is used only to make your plan more conservative.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
