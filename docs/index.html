AI training plans

A plan that starts where you are.

Answer a few questions about your goal, how far you can run today and which days you're free. The coach builds a week-by-week plan, and every session plays straight in the timer.

  • From your first run/walk to a faster 5K. It progresses gradually, with easier weeks built in.
  • Fits your week. Sessions only land on your training days, and never run longer than you have.
  • Learns from your runs. Target paces come from what you've actually run.

Distance & pace

Every interval, measured.

See how far you went and how fast in each work and rest interval, live while you run and as a full breakdown when you finish.

  • GPS outdoors. Filtered so standing still doesn't add phantom distance.
  • Step counter indoors. It takes over on a treadmill or wherever GPS is weak, one stretch at a time.
  • Free runs too. No intervals? Pick Run or Walk for a stopwatch with live distance and pace.
  • Every run in History. Backed up to your account, with each interval kept.

Routes

Know where you're going.

Pick a start and a distance and Runwise makes routes for you, measured along real roads and paths. Or draw your own, one tap at a time.

  • Loop or out & back, with three options heading different ways.
  • Parks and trails first. Routes aim for green space near your start.
  • Matched to your session. Each plan session suggests saved routes that fit how far it'll take you.
  • A live map while you run, with your route traced behind the timer.
