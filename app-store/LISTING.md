## Description [4000]

Runwise is a running coach that plans your weeks, maps your routes and talks you through every run, from your first run/walk intervals to a faster 5K.

TELL IT ABOUT YOUR RUNNING, GET A PLAN
Answer a few questions about your goal, current fitness and the days you can train. Runwise builds a week-by-week plan that starts where you are and progresses gradually, from your first run/walk to a faster 5K. Every session is ready to start with one tap.

A COACH YOU CAN HEAR
• A spoken countdown for the last five seconds of every interval, and a clear tone at each switch
• "Halfway there" and how many intervals are left
• Works with the screen locked, over your music, and with the silent switch on
• Music dips briefly for cues, then comes straight back

DISTANCE FOR EVERY INTERVAL
• GPS outdoors, the step counter on a treadmill or when GPS is weak
• Distance and pace for each interval, spoken after every work interval if you like
• A full breakdown when you finish, saved to your history

ROUTES FOR EVERY RUN
• Pick a start and a distance, and Runwise suggests loops or out-and-backs measured along real roads and paths
• Prefers nearby parks and trails, or draw your own one tap at a time
• A live 3D map follows you as you run

FREE RUNS
• No plan today? Start a run or walk with live distance and pace, and save it to your history

BUILD ANY WORKOUT
• Warm-up, intervals, rounds and cool-down
• Custom patterns like jog 1:30, walk 1:30, jog 3:00, walk 3:00
• Pause, resume and skip, always on time to the second

BACKED UP
Sign in with Apple or your email, and your workouts, history and plan are saved to your account. Everything still works offline.

Training plans are generated by AI and aren't medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise program.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

**Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** Yes.

Select these data types. For every one: **Linked to the user: Yes. Used for tracking: No. Purpose: App Functionality** only.

| Category | Data type | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Contact Info | Email Address | Sign-in email |
| Health & Fitness | Health | Optional injuries / health conditions answer; optional per-interval heart rate (from Apple Health or a Bluetooth monitor) |
| Health & Fitness | Fitness | Workouts, run history, distance and pace, training plans |
| Location | Precise Location | Routes the user draws on the map to plan runs |
| Identifiers | User ID | Account ID |
| User Content | Other User Content | "Anything else your coach should know" notes |
| Other Data | Other Data Types | Age range, running experience, goals, schedule |

**Precise Location** covers saved routes only: the points a user taps on the map when planning a run are synced to their account. GPS and step data recorded during a run stay on the phone; only the distances worked out from them are uploaded. Leave **Coarse Location** unticked. This matches `IntervalRun/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`. Update both together if that changes.

## Age rating

**Notes** (paste this in):

> Runwise is a running coach and planner: AI-generated training plans, route planning, and a voice-guided timer for run/walk and 5K training.
>
> SIGN IN: Please use "Sign in with Apple" on the first screen. (Email sign-in sends a one-time code, which needs an inbox.) After signing in, answer the short onboarding questions. Tapping "Build my plan" creates a training plan with Anthropic's Claude in about a minute. The user agrees to share their answers with Claude on the last onboarding screen before anything is sent.
>
> TRY A WORKOUT: Workouts tab → any workout → Start. Voice cues and beeps play at each interval switch and count down the last 5 seconds.
>
> BACKGROUND AUDIO: the app plays timed beeps and spoken cues for the whole workout, including with the screen locked. That's why it uses the "audio" background mode. It mixes with and briefly ducks other apps' music.
>
> BACKGROUND LOCATION: during a workout only, the app uses GPS to measure each interval's distance, and keeps doing so when the screen is locked, with the blue location indicator showing. It uses "When In Use" permission only. Tracking stops when the workout ends. GPS readings from the run never leave the device; only distances are saved.
>
> ROUTES: Plan tab → map icon (top left) → + to draw a route by tapping the map. Routes the user saves are synced to their account so they're available on their other devices. Or "Create for me": pick a start and a distance and the app suggests routes, preferring nearby parks and trails. Route points are sent to Apple Maps to find walking paths and nearby parks. A plan session's Route section lets the user pick one, and the player then shows it on a map.
>
> HEART RATE: optional. Settings tab → Heart rate monitor connects a standard Bluetooth heart rate monitor (chest strap, armband, or a Garmin watch broadcasting heart rate); that's why the app uses the "bluetooth-central" background mode, so readings continue with the screen locked during a workout. "Read from Apple Health" reads heart rate only (HealthKit read access; nothing is written) for the time of each run. Heart rate is summarized per interval, shown in the run's results and History, saved to the user's account, and included in the run summary sent to Claude for plan building with the user's consent. It is never used for advertising. Without a monitor, the app works fully without heart rate.
>
> ACCOUNT DELETION: Settings tab → Delete account. This permanently deletes the account and all its data. Apple accounts also have their Sign in with Apple tokens revoked.
